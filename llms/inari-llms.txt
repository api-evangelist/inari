# Inari

> Inari is The SEEDesign™ Company. Its SEEDesign™ technology platform combines genomics, artificial intelligence, and multiplex gene editing to unlock the full potential of seed — accelerating crop improvement for higher yield and lower resource use. Inari operates a partner-first, business-to-business model, working with seed companies and breeders rather than selling directly to farmers. Stated goals: 10–20% yield increase, 40% reduction in nitrogen use, and 40% reduction in water use.

Inari Agriculture, Inc. (inari.com) is a gene-editing and predictive plant-design company headquartered in Cambridge, Massachusetts, with operations in West Lafayette, Indiana and Ghent, Belgium. Note: "Inari" here refers to Inari Agriculture (seed/gene editing) — not the unrelated medical-device company Inari Medical.
